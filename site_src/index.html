Active BGP & Probing Outages (IODA)

Median during the event against the median of the 7 days before it, per IODA signal (BGP, active probing, darknet). BO-IND-1: ≥25% for ≥30 min. BO-IND-4: one signal drops while BGP holds.

Application-Layer Censorship (OONI)

Confirmed blocking + anomaly share of all OONI measurements over 7 days, all tests together (BO-IND-3: ≥15% over ≥50 measurements). A fixed threshold, not a change from baseline: some countries sit above it every day.

Suite Tips (Elements, Wake, Vigil)

Interests BO-INT-1 (earthquakes, volcanoes), BO-INT-2 (AIS loitering or anchor drag; Wake is not live yet) and BO-INT-3 (attacks on telecom carriers). A tip counts toward a cable-fault call (BO-IND-5) only when its own coordinates fall within 50 km of a corridor point; tips naming an outage country are shown as context and do not change any likelihood.
